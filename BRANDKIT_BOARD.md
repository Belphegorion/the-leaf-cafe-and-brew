## 3. Logo Anatomy & Geometry
The official identity is vector-constructed with 2px/11px constant monoline strokes:
1. **The Concentric Arch**: Spanning across the upper third, referencing the physical arched windows of the Arera Colony cafe.
2. **The Teardrop Leaf**: Centered vertically with a defined spine and angled branching lateral veins.
3. **The Intersecting Coffee Cup**: Pierced through the leaf base with a distinct rim line, bowl contour, handle, and saucer arc.
4. **The Undulating Waves**: Three horizontal rhythmic waves below the cup, evoking steam, water flow, and slow-steeped extraction.
5. **Art Deco Typography**: Geometric uppercase lettering with wide letter-spacing (`tracking-[0.25em]`).

---

## 4. Typography Scale & Pairings
- **Display & Headings**: `Josefin Sans` & `Poiret One` (Uppercase, geometric Art Deco styling, wide letter-spacing).
- **Body & Controls**: `DM Sans` / `Inter` (Humanist clarity, clean legibility at small sizes).
- **Numerals & Prices**: Tabular figures with Indian Rupee symbol (`₹`).

---

## 5. Touchpoint Matrix & Rules
- **No Random AI Purples**: Never inject generic neon gradients, purple meshes, or template cards.
- **Double-Bezel Architecture**: Cards utilize an outer soft rounded border (16–24px) paired with flat inner backgrounds and subtle layered hover shadows.
- **Accessible Touch Targets**: Every button and clickable element enforces minimum 44×44px hit bounds with visible `:focus-visible` rings.
- **Micro-Interactions**: Spring physics easing `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`, animated coffee cup steppers, and subtle leaf confetti on victory moments.
